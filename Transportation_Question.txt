### Case Study: Optimizing a Company’s Delivery Network

A logistics company operates four warehouses  (S_1,S_2,S_3,S_4) that supply products to **four retail distribution centers (D_1,D_2,D_3,D_4).

The company needs to transport its entire available stock from the warehouses to the distribution centers. 
The transportation cost per unit depends on the warehouse and destination, as shown below:

| Warehouse / Destination |    D₁ |    D₂ |     D₃ |     D₄ | Available Supply |
| ----------------------- | ----: | ----: | -----: | -----: | ---------------: |
| S₁                      |    19 |    30 |     50 |     10 |                7 |
| S₂                      |    70 |    30 |     40 |     60 |                9 |
| S₃                      |    40 |     8 |     70 |     20 |               18 |
| S₄                      |    30 |    25 |     35 |     15 |               16 |
| **Required Demand**     | **5** | **8** | **20** | **17** |                  |

Question:
A logistics company has four warehouses and four distribution centers. The transportation costs, supply, and demand are given below. 
Determine the transportation plan that minimizes the total transportation cost.

Use Vogel’s Approximation Method (VAM) to obtain the initial basic feasible solution and calculate its transportation cost. 
Then apply the **MODI Method** to test and improve the solution until the optimal transportation plan and minimum total cost are obtained. 
Develop a Python program using NumPy to implement VAM and MODI.
